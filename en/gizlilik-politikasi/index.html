2. Which personal data do we process?

When you contact us yourself: full name, email address, phone number and message content (via the contact form, email or telephone).

Automatically when you visit the site: technical records such as IP address, browser and device information, pages visited and time of visit.

Cookies and similar technologies: preference records kept in your browser's local storage. See our Cookie Policy page for details.

Our centre has security cameras (CCTV) ; those recordings relate to physical visits and are outside the scope of this website.

3. Purposes and legal grounds for processing

Responding to your requests and questions — KVKK art. 5/2-c (directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract) and art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest).

Assessing career and leasing applications — KVKK art. 5/2-c and art. 5/2-f.

Ensuring the security and proper operation of the site — KVKK art. 5/2-ç (legal obligation) and art. 5/2-f.

Measuring site usage with optional cookies — only with your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1).

Fulfilling our legal obligations — KVKK art. 5/2-ç.

4. Transfer of personal data

Your personal data is not sold to third parties or shared for marketing purposes. It may be transferred only in the following cases:

  • To the infrastructure and technical service providers hosting the site (within the scope of server logs).
  • To authorised public institutions and organisations where required by law.

As hosting may be provided abroad, technical records may be transferred abroad within the scope of KVKK art. 9.

5. Method of collection

Your personal data is collected through forms on the website, email, telephone and automatic server records, by fully or partly automated means.

6. Retention period

Your data is kept for as long as the purpose of processing requires and for the limitation periods set out in the relevant legislation, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights under KVKK art. 11

In relation to your personal data you have the right to:

  • learn whether it is being processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties to whom it is transferred at home or abroad,
  • request correction if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly,
  • request its deletion or destruction where the conditions are met,
  • request that correction, deletion and destruction be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

You hold these rights.
